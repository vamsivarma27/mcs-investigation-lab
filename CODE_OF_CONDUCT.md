# Code of Conduct

We want this project to be a welcoming, practical place to collaborate.

Be respectful, assume good intent, give actionable feedback, and focus criticism on ideas and
code. Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, sexualized attention, and sustained personal
attacks are not acceptable in project spaces.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content and may temporarily or permanently restrict
participation when needed to protect the community. Report conduct concerns privately to the
maintainer through their GitHub profile. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical.
